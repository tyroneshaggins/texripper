    How to use TexRipper

    Rip textures out of any photo, straighten perspective, make them tile, and pack them into an atlas.

    1. Load an image — Open, drag & drop, or paste. Hit Demo Image to try it instantly.
    2. Shape a ripper — drag the 4 corner handles around a flat surface. Alt+click an edge midpoint to add a point.
    3. Pick a resolution — the rip is perspective-corrected into the output size. Use Match Polygon Size for 1:1 sampling.
    4. Make it seamless — tweak the method, seam blend, contrast, brightness and sharpen. The tiling preview is live.
    5. Arrange the atlas — drag textures inside the atlas, then Export Atlas as a PNG.
